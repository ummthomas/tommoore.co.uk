Personal project

Family Portrait

A visual time capsule of 90s childhood captured in family holidays.

The brief

Objective
Consolidate a collection of digitised family holiday tapes from my childhood into a cohesive short film, designed for easy and enjoyable viewing.
Format
Short film
Context
Personal project, made from digitised family holiday tapes

Introducing Family Portrait

Combining nostalgic, unedited visuals with a narrative that encapsulates my family’s experiences during our 90s holidays, Family Portrait acts as a time capsule, allowing us to reflect on a previous chapter in our lives.

Outcome

This short film is one I often find myself returning to, as it stirs a deep sense of nostalgia and warmth. Each frame is a portal to moments long past—laughter at the beach, road trips filled with spontaneous adventures, and quiet evenings spent together.

Through this exploration of cherished memories, Family Portrait not only highlights the joyful moments we shared but also invites viewers to reconnect with their own pasts. It aims to evoke a sense of belonging, reminding us all of the beauty found in our personal histories.
